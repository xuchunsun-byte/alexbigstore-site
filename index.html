FAQ

Will this find sponsors for me?

No. If you need more brands in the pipeline, use outreach, networks, or a marketplace. This kit is for operating sponsorships you’ve already sold.

Is this an official Beehiiv / Substack partner?

No. It works alongside any publisher stack. We don’t claim platform partnership or auto-insert into newsletter bodies.

How is this different from Sponsy / AdSlots?

Those are fuller sponsorship platforms (often from ~$49/mo up, with more automation surface). The Ops Kit is a $29 Airtable/Notion template for calendar + creatives + invoice status + reports. Ops Seat aims at a light $19–29/mo band if you outgrow duplicating bases.

Do you guarantee revenue or CPM?

No. No fabricated ROI. Outcomes depend on your audience, rates, and how you run deals.
