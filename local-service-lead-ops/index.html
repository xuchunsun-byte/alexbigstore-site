No. The pack automates response, follow-up, and deposit reminders for leads you already get. Volume and show-rates still depend on your marketing, pricing, and crew.

Is this a Jobber / Housecall Pro replacement?

No. Those are full field-service suites. This is a lighter n8n automation pack for lead → follow-up → deposit only—useful if you don’t want another ~$200/mo SaaS for that slice.

n8n Cloud or self-hosted?

Both. Workflows are standard n8n JSON. SETUP covers Cloud and self-hosted credential notes. You run (or subscribe to) n8n yourself—we don’t provide managed hosting.

What about SMS / email costs and compliance?

You use your own Twilio (or SMTP). Per-message fees are yours. You must follow TCPA/consent and CAN-SPAM rules; get opt-in before texting; respect quiet hours.

I’m not technical—can I still use this?

If you can follow a checklist and paste API keys, yes. Never opened n8n? Budget an evening or have a VA who’s done one n8n import help once.
