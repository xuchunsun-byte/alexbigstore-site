Thanks — here’s how to install Trade Lead First-Reply

Your unique license key and the extension ZIP are delivered by Creem in your receipt email and in your Creem customer portal. Check spam if you don’t see the email within a few minutes.

  • 1. Copy your license key

    Find it in your purchase email or in your Creem customer portal.

  • 2. Download the extension ZIP

    Use the file download in the same receipt email or customer portal. Unzip it and keep the folder somewhere permanent (don’t delete it after loading).

Install in Chrome

  1. Open Chrome and go to chrome://extensions.
  2. Turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder that contains manifest.json.
  4. Open Extension options, paste your license key, and Save. Add or edit your HVAC pricebook ranges.
  5. In Gmail, open a lead email from Angi, Thumbtack, Google Local Services, or HomeAdvisor. Click Draft first reply, review the draft, then send it yourself.
This early build is installed in Chrome Developer mode until it is listed on the Chrome Web Store. The extension only inserts a draft—it never sends email for you. Full steps also live in INSTALL.md inside the ZIP.

Trade Lead First-Reply is not affiliated with Angi, Thumbtack, Google, or HomeAdvisor. Those names only describe where lead-notification emails may come from. Questions about install or the license key: reply to your purchase email, or write xuchunsun@gmail.com with your Chrome version (do not include customer PII in screenshots).
