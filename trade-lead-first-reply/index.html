FAQ

Will this email the homeowner for me?

No. It only inserts a draft into Gmail’s reply box. Sending is always manual. We never auto-send, and we do not scrape Angi or Thumbtack pages to message leads.

Where do the dollar amounts come from?

Your pricebook. Drafts are Fact-locked to those rows—the extension should not invent prices that aren’t in your table.

Does it work outside Gmail?

This version is Gmail-only.

Is this a generic ChatGPT sidebar?

No. The button shows only on recognized lead emails from Angi, Thumbtack, Google LSA, and HomeAdvisor patterns (source-of-lead mail only).

Do you guarantee more booked HVAC jobs?

No. Faster, clearer first replies help you compete on shared leads; results still depend on your pricing, reviews, and capacity. We don’t invent testimonials or win-rate stats.

Are you affiliated with Angi, Thumbtack, Google, or HomeAdvisor?

No. Those names appear only to describe where lead-notification emails may come from. Trade Lead First-Reply is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Angi, Thumbtack, Google, or HomeAdvisor.

How do I install it after paying?

When purchase opens, you’ll get a license key and the extension ZIP. Unzip, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, Load unpacked, then paste the key in Options. Full steps will be in INSTALL.md inside the ZIP.
